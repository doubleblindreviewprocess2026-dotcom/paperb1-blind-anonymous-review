Interactive demos

Five views of the data. Each one reads a JSON file that is built from the CSV files named under it, and says how many points it shows. Nothing is computed here that is not in a CSV file, except sums, means and the parabola fits that the CSV files also report.

1. Reaction paths: HCN → HNC, NH₃ inversion, H₂CO → H₂ + CO

Energy along a relaxed path (B3LYP-D3(BJ) geometries, def2-TZVP), relative to the first point. The black crosses are the coupled-cluster reference; coloured symbols are the functionals (their energy is the reference plus their signed error). Move the slider to step along the path and see the geometry.

2. SN2 symmetric stretch: where is the transition state of each method?

Both C–X bonds are stretched together (the rest of the molecule fixed). For a D₃h SN2 transition state this symmetric stretch has the transition state as a minimum, so the lowest point of each curve is that method's barrier, measured from the ion-molecule complex. Open symbols are calculations whose SCF did not converge: they are shown but not used. The slider steps through the calculated distances.

3. HCN T1 map: where is the coupled-cluster reference reliable?

T1 diagnostic of CCSD(T)/def2-SVP on a rigid grid: angle θ (0° = H on the carbon side) against distance r of H from the centre of mass of CN. Cells above the chosen T1 threshold are orange. Cells more than 120 kcal/mol above the lowest grid energy (outside the "window") are faded. The usual flag levels are 0.02 and 0.03.

4. Cost against accuracy

Median wall time of one single-point calculation against the mean absolute error of the five-reaction headline set. Wall times are from one run of the GPU notebook (Tesla T4) and indicate order of magnitude, not a benchmark.
