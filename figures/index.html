The script that draws the figures is in figures/code/; see Code: rebuild the figures.

Colour-vision checks of the figures (simulated deuteranopia and grey scale) were made for the paper; the symbols differ in shape as well as colour. Figure files are listed with checksums in SHA256SUMS.txt.
