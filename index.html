Skala-1.1 on reaction barriers and reaction paths

This site holds the data, code and interactive views behind a study of the learned density functional Skala-1.1 on the barriers and reaction paths of five small reactions: HCN → HNC, NH₃ inversion, H₂CO → H₂ + CO, F⁻ + CH₃F and Cl⁻ + CH₃Cl. Against literature barriers (n = 5 reactions, B3LYP-D3(BJ) geometries, def2-TZVP or def2-TZVPD), Skala-1.1 has a mean absolute error of 1.44 kcal/mol, against 1.08 for ωB97M-V, 2.26 for B3LYP-D3(BJ), 3.29 for r²SCAN and 4.52 for PBE.

The signed error of Skala-1.1 is positive for four reactions (+0.30 to +1.91 kcal/mol) and negative for Cl⁻ + CH₃Cl (−3.12), while PBE, r²SCAN and B3LYP-D3(BJ) put all five barriers too low. Five reactions are a small sample: the numbers describe these reactions only, and the limitations are listed on the About page.

How to read the numbers

  • Every error on this site is computed minus reference, in kcal/mol. A positive error means the computed barrier is too high.
  • All energies are single points at B3LYP-D3(BJ) geometries optimised for this study (def2-TZVP; def2-TZVPD for the two SN2 reactions). The exceptions are labelled.
  • Every number comes from a CSV file in data. The interactive pages read JSON files that are built from those CSV files by tools/build_site.py.
