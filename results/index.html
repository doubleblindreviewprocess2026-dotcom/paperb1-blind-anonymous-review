Where the numbers come from

  • Barriers and errors: data/csv/study/master_errors.csv (one row per scored comparison; this page uses the rows with is_primary_barrier = True).
  • Errors against our own coupled-cluster value: derived_internal_reference.csv. H₂CO is missing there: the CCSD(T) calculation at its transition state is unreliable (see Checks).
  • Reverse barriers, reaction energies and SN2 barriers from separated reactants are in master_errors.csv and tableS1_all_scored_rows.csv.
